Privacy policy

Chief of Staff VPS · Effective October 7, 2026

Purpose and information accessed

This personal integration is operated by Danil Retunsky for his own use. With his authorization, it accesses Google Drive file names, identifiers, metadata and requested file contents to support search, retrieval, summaries and Chief of Staff work. It can create selected new files through the application in a designated private storage area.

Use and processing

Only information relevant to the owner’s requested tasks is intended to be retrieved. Retrieved content may be sent to Hermes and its configured AI provider, currently OpenAI, for inference. Processing is subject to the applicable provider’s terms and data controls. This integration does not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored in a private credential directory on the operator’s VPS, outside the knowledge vault and unavailable to the conversational assistant. File metadata, task outputs and operational records may be retained in the operator’s private systems. Working downloads are intended to be temporary and bounded. No Google user data or credentials are published on this website.

Sharing

Data is processed by Google Drive, the operator’s hosting infrastructure, and the configured AI provider when required for authorized tasks. It is not sold, used for advertising or intentionally shared with unrelated parties. The integration does not create public file links or change Drive sharing permissions.

Retention and control

The owner controls retained files and outputs and may stop the integration or revoke its access through Google Account security settings. Revocation stops future authorized access; it does not automatically erase previously retained local outputs or Drive files. The operator can remove retained copies and credentials separately.

Limited use

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
